Tax-free threshold

Choose yes or no

What is the tax-free threshold?

The first $18,200 you earn each financial year isn't taxed. Claiming it here means less tax comes out of each Bainz pay.

Claim it from one job only, usually the one that pays you the most. If you already claim it at another job, choose No, or you could owe tax at the end of the year.

Foreign residents and working holiday makers can't claim it.

What counts as a study or training loan?

Safety at Bainz

Read this before your first shift. If you get hurt at work, tell a manager straight away and put it in writing the same day.

Our job and yours

Bainz must give you a safe workplace and the information, training and supervision to work safely. That includes your mental health.

Under the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic) you must:

  • take reasonable care of your own health and safety and everyone around you
  • follow reasonable safety instructions and Bainz procedures
  • not misuse or interfere with anything there for safety
  • report hazards, incidents and near misses straight away

If you get hurt or something happens

  1. Get first aid or medical help first.
  2. Tell the manager or senior on shift before you leave that day.
  3. Fill in the incident report the same day. If you can't, a manager fills it in with you.
  4. It goes in the Register of Injuries kept at Bainz.
  5. Report near misses too, even when nobody was hurt.

WorkSafe Victoria requires you to tell your employer in writing as soon as possible, and within 30 days, to stay eligible for compensation. The WorkSafe "If you are injured at work" poster at Bainz explains claims and return to work. You will never be punished for reporting a real injury or hazard. Injuries that happen outside work must not be reported as work injuries.

Emergencies

  • Fire, gas leak or evacuation: stop work, turn off gas and equipment if it's safe, leave by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point.
  • Call 000 in an emergency, then tell the manager.
  • On your first shift your supervisor shows you the exits, assembly point, first aid kit, fire extinguisher and fire blanket.

Café hazards

HazardWhat you do
Burns and scaldsPurge the steam wand away from you. Use oven gloves. Call "hot behind". Cool a burn under running water for 20 minutes.
CutsCut on a stable board, away from you. Never catch a falling knife. Sweep broken glass, never pick it up. Slicers only after training.
Slips and tripsClean spills straight away and put out the wet floor sign. Wear enclosed non-slip shoes. Keep walkways and exits clear.
LiftingBend your knees, keep the load close, split heavy loads, use the trolley or ask for help.
ChemicalsUse as labelled and never mix. Gloves and eye protection when decanting. Ask where the safety data sheets are.
ElectricalReport damaged cords or plugs. No wet hands on equipment. Unplug before cleaning.
Fatigue and heatTake your break on shifts over 5 hours. Drink water. Tell your supervisor if you feel unwell.
Aggressive customersStay calm and don't argue. Step back and get the manager. Never physically remove anyone. Call 000 if anyone is at risk.

Respect at work

Bullying, sexual harassment and discrimination are not tolerated, from staff or customers. Raise a concern with Tyler or Linda (if it's about one of them, go to the other). It's handled confidentially and you won't be treated badly for raising it.

Food safety

  • Complete DoFoodSafely before handling unpackaged ready-to-eat food, and give us the certificate.
  • Wash your hands before starting and after breaks, bins, raw food and the bathroom.
  • Don't work with food within 48 hours of vomiting or diarrhoea. Tell the manager.
  • Unsure about allergens? Ask a chef. Never guess.

Alcohol

Serve only people 18 or over and never anyone who seems intoxicated. Ask for ID if unsure.
